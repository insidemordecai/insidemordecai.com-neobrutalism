Welcome back to **Dispatch**, a personal roundup of videos, records, and anything that I found interesting this month. Think of **Dispatch** as my monthly mixtape from the internet with no real theme except “this was neat.”

## Videos 
- I read Ghost In The Wire by Kevin Mitnick a long long time ago but that is easily the most borrowed book in my "home library " among my techie friends. Kevin is famous for phreaking in the 80s with fern describes him in this video as [the kid who hacked America](https://youtu.be/dNGUiWKn6Qs). 
- Christopher Nolan's *The Odyssey* has rightfully taken the internet by a storm and this is a great look into how a [projectionist handles 70mm film](https://youtu.be/tN05nzk9oT8). The process is quite intricate and very analog. Come to think of it, Nolan is singlehandedly ensuring such a profession remains active.
- [Speeed](https://www.youtube.com/@SpeeedCo) has been described a a video version of a men's magazine and it's 100% true. In this video, James Pumphrey [breaks down wristwatches](https://youtu.be/AdJkqGo_9Qk): the vocabulary, how they work, the history, what to look out for. The video is quite interesting even for non-beginners.
- Did you know that at some point most countries tried to agree on a road signs standard? If you're like me and aren't aware of that, then this video looks into [why the world rejected the UN's road signs](https://youtu.be/MesMQ6t9ODM) and how they came to settle with what is currently in place. 
- Many people woefully underestimate [how much it takes financially to get into Formula 1](https://youtu.be/xgj_lMeclNw). It recently became clear that there is a Kenyan F4 racer seeking support from sponsors but a lot of online users (and friends) simply brushed it off because the kid is from one of the wealthiest families in the country. This video might shed a light for those new to the world of F1. 
- I often try to avoid videos that are even slightly political but [Max Fisher](https://www.youtube.com/@maxfisher) has a great coverage of the fuel situation owing to the ongoing war in the Middle East. In this video, he shows how [China silently saved the world last month](https://youtu.be/BkA0bkb6ZO0) staving off the projected fuel crisis.
